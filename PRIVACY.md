BrightView does not collect, transmit, sell, or store personal data externally.

All data processed by BrightView is accessed locally within the user’s browser and is limited to grade information already visible to the user on Brightspace/D2L pages. Any calculations or summaries are performed entirely on the user’s device.

BrightView does not use analytics, tracking, remote servers, or third-party services. Any settings stored using chrome.storage.local remain on the user’s device and are never accessible to the developer.

If BrightView is uninstalled, all locally stored data is removed automatically.
